Face Guard privacy policy

Effective September 23, 2026

The short version

Face Guard uses your webcam to notice when your hand touches your face. All video is processed on your own device, in memory, and immediately discarded. No images, video, or face or hand data are ever saved, uploaded, or shared. Face Guard has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What Face Guard processes

  • Camera frames. While Face Guard is watching, it reads frames from your camera about ten times a second. It runs on-device machine-learning models on each frame to find face and hand positions, and cups or bottles near your face. Frames are never written to disk or sent anywhere. The camera turns off when you pause Face Guard, lock your PC, or quit the app.
  • Settings and daily statistics. Your preferences and simple counts (such as how many times you touched your face each day) are stored locally on your device. They never leave it. Uninstalling Face Guard can remove them.

Network use

The installed app works fully offline. Face Guard only connects to the internet if one of its bundled machine-learning model files is missing. In that case it downloads the file from Google's public MediaPipe model storage (storage.googleapis.com). That request contains no personal data.

Permissions

  • Camera: to detect face touches. Face Guard never requests your microphone.
  • Notifications: to alert you.
  • Start with Windows (optional): so it can keep watching after you sign in. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Sign out / lock (optional, off by default): if you turn on Consequences, Face Guard can sign you out of Windows or lock your PC after repeated alerts. It shows a countdown first.

Children

Face Guard does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the app and on this page.
